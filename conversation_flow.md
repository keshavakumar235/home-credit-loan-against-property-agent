# Conversation Flow — Home Credit LAP Voice Agent

## 1. Objective

The voice agent contacts existing Home Credit customers to discuss a potential Loan Against Property (LAP) offer of up to ₹75,00,000. It performs preliminary qualification and routes eligible customers to a senior loan expert.

The agent does not approve loans, guarantee eligibility, or promise specific interest rates.

## 2. Call Flow

### Step 1: Greeting and Customer Verification
- Greet the customer naturally.
- Confirm that the intended customer is speaking before disclosing offer details.
- If the person is not the intended customer, avoid revealing financial information and end politely.

### Step 2: Check Availability
- Ask whether it is a convenient time to talk.
- If the customer is busy, ask for a preferred callback time.
- Acknowledge the response and end the call without continuing qualification.

### Step 3: Introduce the Offer
- Explain that Home Credit has a potential Loan Against Property offer of up to ₹75 lakh.
- Clarify that this is an initial qualification conversation, not final loan approval.
- Ask whether the customer is interested in discussing the offer.

### Step 4: Identify Special Routing Requirements
- If the customer already has a Loan Against Property with Home Credit, or primarily wants to reduce the EMI on an existing loan, route them to a specialist.
- Explain that the relevant team will contact them.
- End the call without continuing the new-loan qualification process.

### Step 5: Preliminary Qualification Checklist

The agent must obtain answers to all seven checklist items before handing the customer to a senior expert.

| No. | Checklist item | Preliminary eligibility rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Property type | Residential, commercial, or industrial property is eligible for further screening. Agricultural property is disqualified. |
| 2 | Ownership | Sole or joint ownership is acceptable. |
| 3 | Original property documents | The customer must have the original property documents available. |
| 4 | Desired loan amount | The requested amount must not exceed ₹75,00,000. If it is higher, explain the cap and ask whether the customer wants to proceed with ₹75 lakh. |
| 5 | Occupation and income | Salaried and self-employed customers may proceed if their income is received through a bank. Cash-only income is disqualifying. |
| 6 | Estimated property market value | Ask for the customer's estimated property value and record the answer. Do not invent a minimum value threshold. |
| 7 | Desired tenure | The requested tenure must be between 3 and 15 years, inclusive. |

### Step 6: Handle Answers and Disqualification
- Accept relevant answers even if the customer provides them out of order.
- Remember previously provided answers and avoid asking the same question again unnecessarily.
- Ask a clear follow-up question when an answer is incomplete or ambiguous.
- If a disqualifying condition is confirmed, explain the relevant reason politely and end the qualification process.
- If the requested amount exceeds ₹75 lakh, offer the customer the option to continue with ₹75 lakh. Proceed only if the customer agrees.

### Step 7: Final Qualification Gate
Before handoff, verify that:
- All seven checklist items have been answered.
- No disqualifying condition has been identified.
- The requested amount is within the offer cap, including any customer-agreed adjustment to ₹75 lakh.
- The customer is interested in proceeding.

If any checklist item is unanswered, ask for that information instead of handing off.

### Step 8: Successful Handoff
- Summarize the customer's responses briefly.
- Explain that a senior loan expert will contact the customer to discuss the next steps and exact rates.
- Avoid promising loan approval or a specific rate.
- Close the conversation politely.

## 3. Conversation Quality and Safety
- Use natural, concise, conversational language.
- Allow the customer to interrupt or ask questions.
- Do not pressure the customer to proceed.
- Do not invent customer information, eligibility rules, or loan terms.
- Protect customer privacy and avoid disclosing offer details before verification.
- If the customer declines, acknowledge the decision and end politely.
- If the customer asks a question that cannot be answered reliably, explain that a loan expert can clarify it.

## 4. Important Limitation

This flow performs preliminary qualification only. Final eligibility, valuation, documentation checks, interest rates, and loan approval remain subject to Home Credit's applicable processes.
